Noodle Recipe

What You Need

  • 500 grams of wheat
  • eggs
  • meat
  • green plant
  • Gochujang

Steps

  1. make a flour
  2. boil the flour
  3. Warning: use medium heat when boiling the noodle.
  4. Place noodle in a bowl; arrange vegetables and egg on top.
  5. Add gochujang and sesame oil, then egg on top of it.
